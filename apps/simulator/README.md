# Home simulator

A pretend household for the real Home Assistant, with a 3D house that shows what is happening and lets you click devices.

It replaces the old "drifting sensors" script. Home Assistant still holds the devices (template lights, switches, fans, locks and sensors backed by helpers, see `ha/home_package.yaml`, generated by `ha/gen.py`); this app plays the world around them.

## Run it

```
HA_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8123 HA_TOKEN=<long-lived token> bun run dev:simulator   # from the repo root
```

Open http://localhost:3100. To reach it from outside (a tunnel), set `SIM_KEY=<long random text>`: nothing works without it, and `/?key=<that text>` signs a browser in once. The page can switch locks and plugs in Home Assistant, so never expose it without a key. Optional: `SPEED=60` to start fast, `SEED=7` to replay the same days.

## What it simulates

- **Time**: starts at the real time. Speed buttons: real time, 10x, 60x, 300x, 1200x, pause. Energy counters add up in simulated time, so a fast clock moves them fast. Home Assistant's own history is stamped with real time, so keep fast clocks short.
- **Weather**: outdoor temperature and humidity follow the day, cloud cover and wind wander, the sun sets the solar output.
- **Heat and air**: each room warms and cools from the weather, sun, people, lights and appliances. Open windows and doors leak heat. The heating and cooling follow `climate.ecobee` (the thermostat in your app). CO2 and humidity rise with people, showers and cooking.
- **People**: Maya, Dev, Kiran and Nani follow weekday and weekend routines with a different offset each day. They walk through the hallway, switch lights on when a room is dark, and usually switch them off after leaving (about 1 time in 14 they forget). They use the kettle, coffee machine, dishwasher, TV and office plugs, lock and unlock the front door, and Dev's car charges when he is home.
- **Power**: every plug has its own profile (fridge compressor cycles, kettle boils and switches off, dishwasher runs a real cycle, the car charges and tapers). Whole-home power is what is actually on, plus heating. Solar follows the sun and cloud.
- **Detail you can see**: light brightness (and a glow that follows it), ceiling-fan and exhaust-fan speed, the wall thermostat in the hallway with its real reading and target, ceiling vents that glow orange or blue while heating or cooling, windows that slide open by their position, a garage door that rolls up, pergola slats that turn, the TV playing, clouds that thicken with the cloud cover, small tags on big loads, and people who sit on the sofa, stools and desk chairs, and lie in bed.
- **Habits that use those controls**: lights come on at a level that suits the time of day (dimmer late), the TV dims the floor lamp, the bedroom fan picks 33, 67 or 100 percent from the room's temperature, the bathroom fan runs flat out during a shower, the thermostat is lowered overnight and set back when everyone is out, and the garage door opens for Dev's car.
- **Two-way**: undoing a fault in the app ends it and the 3D house reacts (lock the front door and it swings shut, close the kitchen, living-room or hall window cover, close the garage door, switch the plug off). Opening a window or the garage door from the app changes the heat in that room.
- **Faults**: about one every 8 to 28 simulated hours, or press a button: front door left ajar, window left open, bathroom leak, burnt toast, a plug drawing too much, fridge door left open, garage door left open.

## Not simulated

- A device going offline (the template entities have no availability switch).
- Rain, seasons, a changing indoor layout, or more than one floor.
- The two cameras and the demo media players, which stay as Home Assistant's demo integration makes them.
